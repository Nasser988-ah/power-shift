About

A studio in Cairo that builds software, not decks.

Who you are talking to

POWER SHIFT is a small software studio. When you message us, you reach the people who will design and build the work — not a sales queue.

Where we sit

Based in Cairo. We deliver for Egypt, the GCC, and international clients. Arabic and English are part of the product, not an afterthought.

Who we build for

Primarily small and mid-size businesses. Also founders and teams who need a serious counterpart for a site, a shop, or an operations system.

After launch

Maintenance is part of the offer. The work is specified so it can be changed later — not thrown over the wall.

Questions
