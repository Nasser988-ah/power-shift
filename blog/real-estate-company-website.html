A real estate company website is not a property portal

Buyers need to know who you are and how to ask. A public portal, a company site, and the software your team uses every day are three different jobs.

Nasser Ahmed Published 21 November 2026 Updated 21 November 2026

Strategy

A real estate company website is not a property portal

“We need a real estate website” usually hides three products. One is a company site. One is a public board of listings. One is software the team uses to track units, viewings, and follow-up. Quoting them as the same job is how projects stall.

The company site answers who you are

A buyer, or an owner with a unit to list, wants the areas you work in, the kind of property, and a way to ask. They do not need every unit in the city on day one. A clear page, a phone number, and a form or WhatsApp path are a complete first version. That is still a business website.

A portal is a catalogue with rules

Search, filters, maps, and a listing for every unit is a different scope. It needs photos, statuses, and someone whose job is to keep the board current. If the board will be wrong within a month, do not build it yet. Send people to enquire instead.

Operations software is not the public site

If staff log in every morning to see what is available, who visited, and what was promised, you are not designing a homepage. You are designing a system. Availio is that kind of tool for property operations. It is not a brokerage website, and it should not be presented as one. The difference is the same one in a website or custom software.

Arabic, English, and the Gulf buyer

If the reader might be in the Gulf as well as in Egypt, language is part of the product, not a later translation. A bilingual site is its own build. A single language is an honest first version when the other audience is not real yet.

Write down which of the three products you need this quarter. Then we can scope the website or the system without mixing them.
