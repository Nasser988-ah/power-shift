VIA Holidays — Travel Booking Platform

VIA Holidays needed one place for Czech travellers booking Egypt, Jordan, and Türkiye — tours, transfers, and enquiries — rather than scattered pages and messages.

Open the live VIA Holidays website Book a Scope Call

The Challenge

A travel operator needed one place for Czech travellers booking Egypt, Jordan, and Türkiye — tours, transfers, and enquiries.

Goals

  • Give travellers a single destination for Egypt, Jordan, and Türkiye holidays.
  • Make tours, transfers, and VIP holidays easy to browse.
  • Create a clear path to enquire without a confusing booking maze.

The Solution

A Czech-and-English site for destinations, transfers, and VIP holidays, with a clear path to enquire.

Technology Stack

A custom marketing website with Czech and English content, destination and transfer pages, and an enquiry path. We do not publish a vendor stack for this project.

Key Features

  • Destination pages for Egypt, Jordan, and Türkiye.
  • Transfer and VIP holiday information in one place.
  • Czech and English content for travellers from Czechia and beyond.
  • A direct enquiry path instead of a generic contact dump.

Results

The product is live at viaholidays.eu. We do not publish traffic or conversion figures for this project.
