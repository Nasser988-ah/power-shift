About Power Shift — Cairo Software Studio

This is the official website of POWER SHIFT, a software studio founded by Nasser Ahmed in Cairo, Egypt. We design and build digital products for companies across Egypt, the GCC, and international markets.

Book a Scope Call See Our Work

Who we are

A small studio with direct responsibility

Power Shift works as one team from discovery and design through engineering, launch, and maintenance. Clients speak with the people responsible for the product, not a separate sales queue.

What we build

Websites, commerce, SaaS, and operations software

Our work includes bilingual websites, e-commerce platforms, multi-user SaaS products, booking tools, inventory, invoicing, CRM, and custom systems used by teams every day.

Where we work

Remote-first from Cairo, serving Egypt and the GCC

Power Shift is a remote-first software studio based in Cairo. We work online with clients throughout Egypt, across the GCC, and internationally; we do not operate a public walk-in office.

Founder

Nasser Ahmed

Nasser Ahmed founded Power Shift and remains directly involved in defining and delivering the studio’s work.
